## Production chunking (20 meetings via `process_item`)

- 10,161 segments. Median 12 words (p25 4, p75 24). 32% are ≤5 words and
  0.9% are ≥100 words.
- Boundary kinds: punct 8,218, semantic 1,943, hard_cut 0.
- AMI/ICSI (short turns): punct path, medians 5–13 words.
- Committee (long turns): the punct path is rejected (a piece exceeds 120 s),
  so the semantic path runs, median 24 words. Without the semantic embedder
  this would be a 155-word hard cut.
- Root cause: `chunk()` splits at every gap/punct/semantic boundary and only
  enforces ≤120 s. There is no merge step. The 60 s/170-word target applies
  only to the `_hard_cut` fallback.

## Other observations

- Lexical `websearch_to_tsquery` (AND) gives 0 hits on 30/30 raw questions,
  both on sentences and on ~150-word chunks. OR matching gives hits on all 30.
- DeepSeek emits parallel tool calls despite `parallel_tool_calls=False`. Each
  skipped call still counts toward `tool_calls_limit` (60 skipped vs 108
  executed). The output-token limit was hit in 10/30 cases.
- The retrieval phase leaves DeepSeek thinking on (44 reasoning tokens on a
  trivial first step).
- When several turns share one event loop, an aborted section stream's async
  generator was finalized in another task. It cancelled the next turn
  ("Attempted to exit cancel scope in a different task"). Not reproduced in
  isolation.
- `ZhipuEmbedder` lets `http.client.IncompleteRead` escape unwrapped. In the
  worker, every embedding failure permanently fails the item (no retry).
- Vector search: global HNSW on `segment.embedding` (pgvector 0.8.6 on the
  test branch). An item-scoped query plans an exact sort; a tenant-wide plan
  may differ.
